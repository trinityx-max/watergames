How to surf

  1. Wait for a set. The swell lines show up behind you.
  2. Paddle as the wave lifts you: hold W.
  3. Pop up when it says so: Space.
  4. Ride the face ahead of the whitewater. Turn hard off the top and bottom to score.
ADCarve left / right · spin in the air
WPaddle · pump for speed
SStall / tighter turns, wait for the barrel
SpacePop up · ollie off the lip
ShiftGrab the rail in the air
CCycle camera
EscPause

Gamepad works too: left stick, A to pop, triggers to pump and stall.
